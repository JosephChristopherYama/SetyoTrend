Features of SetyoTrend

AI-Powered Personal Stylist

Your style, your rules. Our AI gets to know your preferences, learns your unique vibe, and suggests outfits that aren’t just about fitting your size—they match your personality too. Whether you’re dressing for a casual day out, a formal event, or just staying cozy, the app helps you nail your look. Bonus? It uses your current wardrobe to create new combinations, so you always feel fresh without having to buy more.

Virtual Closet

Ever wished your wardrobe could organize itself? Now it can. Meet your new digital closet—a space where every item you own is categorized, tagged, and always at your fingertips. No more standing in front of your wardrobe wondering what to wear. Plus, our AI takes things up a notch by suggesting killer combos for any occasion, so you’re never short of inspiration.

Personalized Avatars

Why guess how something will look when you can see it on you? Create a 3D avatar that looks like you, down to the last detail. From trying on new clothes to checking how different sizes will fit, your avatar makes shopping stress-free and fun. Say goodbye to returns and hello to perfect fits.

Fashion Clubs

Fashion is always better with friends. Join Fashion Clubs to connect with like-minded style enthusiasts, swap ideas, and share your favorite looks. It’s like having a 24/7 style squad. Whether you’re into streetwear, high fashion, or DIY trends, there’s a club waiting for you.

E-Commerce Integration

Tired of scrolling through endless options online? We get it. That’s why our app brings together outfits that match your style, fit your size, and are ready to shop in one place. Found something you love? Just tap to buy. No hassle, no second-guessing—just fashion made easy.

Fashion Feed

Think of this as your personal style magazine. Scroll through a feed full of trends, outfit ideas, and inspirations curated just for you. Save your favorite looks, discover new styles, and keep your creativity flowing. It’s like Pinterest and Instagram had a stylish baby—except it knows exactly what you like.

AI Chatbot

Meet your new fashion BFF. Need help picking an outfit for a big event? Looking for a specific style? Our chatbot is here to help, anytime you need. It’s like having a personal stylist in your pocket—just ask, and it delivers.

Size & Fit Guarantee

Imagine shopping with zero doubts. With your personalized avatar and advanced size-checking features, you’ll always know if something will fit before you buy it. No more wasting time on returns or getting disappointed with the wrong size. Just shop confidently and enjoy the perfect fit, every time.

Cross-Platform Access

Your style doesn’t have to stay in one place. Access your wardrobe, outfits, and preferences from anywhere—whether you’re on your phone between classes, on your laptop at home, or switching between devices. Everything stays synced, so you’re always ready to slay.
